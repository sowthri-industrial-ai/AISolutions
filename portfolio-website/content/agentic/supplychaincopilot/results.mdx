## Eval numbers

Measured against a golden set of 240 historical flagged POs across the
three customers, where the "right answer" is the action the procurement
manager actually took (or, where they took the wrong action and it
later cost the line, the action they later said they should have taken).

| Metric | Baseline | With copilot |
|---|---|---|
| Recommendation accuracy | 0.71 | 0.92 |
| Median time-to-decision | 38 min | 4 min |
| Stockouts attributable to PO delays (per quarter) | 12 | 3 |
| Cost per recommendation | n/a | $0.018 |

## Before and after

Before the copilot, the typical flagged PO sat in the buyer queue for
roughly half a working day before a procurement manager even saw it.
By then, two of the three "obvious" alternate suppliers had moved on
to other customers' orders. After: same PO surfaces with a one-screen
brief inside four minutes. The manager approves, edits, or rejects in
under thirty seconds in the median case.

> "The first week I thought it was getting the easy ones and leaving
> me the hard ones. By the third week I noticed the hard ones were
> the ones flagged for review — which is exactly what I want it
> doing."
>
> — Procurement Manager, Customer B

The eval score of 92 in `project.json` is the recommendation accuracy
number from the table, frozen at the last weekly eval run.
