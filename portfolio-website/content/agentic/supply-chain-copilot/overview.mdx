## The problem

Two-week supplier delays were causing recurring stockouts on a $200M
annual procurement budget. Buyers spent four hours per incident
reconciling inventory burn rate, lead times, and alternative supplier
capacity.

## What the system does

- Tracks burn rate against live inventory
- Queries alternative suppliers on detected delay
- Synthesizes a split-order recommendation with cost trade-off

## Outcome

Time-to-decision dropped from 4h to 11 minutes. The pilot line
avoided `$1.4M` in stockout-driven expedites over Q3 and surfaced
two single-source risks the team did not previously track.

> Operator-in-the-loop by design. The agent proposes; a human buyer
> approves before any PO is cut. Auto-approve is gated by confidence ×
> dollar threshold.
